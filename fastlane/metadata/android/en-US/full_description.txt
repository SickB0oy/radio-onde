Radio Onde brings tens of thousands of radio stations from all over the world into one simple, fast, ad-free app.

EXPLORE
• The radio globe: spin the Earth, zoom in on a country and tap a dot to listen to the station located there.
• More than 40 music styles: pop, rock, jazz, electronic, classical, 80s, news, sport…
• Zapping: swipe up to jump from one station to the next, like changing channels.
• Trending stations in your country and suggestions based on what you listen to.
• Search the whole directory, or add a station by its stream address.

LISTEN
• Pause live radio and pick up where you left off.
• Volume leveling: no more jumps in loudness when you switch stations.
• Current title and artist, with a history of everything you heard.
• Radio alarm clock and sleep timer.
• Background playback, lock-screen controls, Bluetooth and Android Auto.

RESPECTFUL
• No ads, no tracking, no account.
• Your favorites, history and tastes stay on your phone.
• Suggestions are computed on the device.

Stations come from the community-run Radio-Browser directory. Globe outlines come from Natural Earth. Logos and streams belong to their respective stations.

Note: the app interface is currently in French.
